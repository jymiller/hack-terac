# Product contract

## Fixed business boundary

The product is a standalone administrative control surface for a
service-delivery business. It is intended to lower cost per trusted outcome
while preserving the client's service contract.

It is not an Enid feature, does not connect to Enid, and has no client data.

## Working synthesis — not locked

The current agent proposal is a cost-quality control loop that may route a
synthetic unit of work through automation, purchased Terac judgment, or HOLD.
A cheaper policy would be evaluated against an explicit quality, coverage, and
turnaround floor before activation.

This synthesis is deliberately non-binding. The authoritative open questions
are recorded in [requirements.json](../.hackathon/requirements.json).

## Human authority boundary

A Terac participant may provide bounded evidence such as AGREE, DISAGREE, or
INSUFFICIENT. The participant does not certify compliance, authorize delivery,
modify policy, or see any client information.
